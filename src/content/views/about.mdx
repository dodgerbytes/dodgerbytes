# About DodgerBytes

DodgerBytes is an independent, fan-run Dodgers news account. The goal is simple: when something happens with the Dodgers, you hear it here first.

## What you'll find here

- **Rumors**, always credited to the reporter who broke them, and labeled RUMOR, REPORT or PREDICTION so you know exactly how solid they are
- **Transactions**: signings, trades, call-ups, IL moves and option decisions, straight from MLB's official feed
- **Free agency**: who's leaving, who's coming, and who's linked to LA
- **Game recaps, awards and "On this day"** moments from recent Dodgers history

## How it works

Most stories start on the DodgerBytes TikTok and Discord, where an automated news desk watches MLB's official data and trusted reporters around the clock. The site is the permanent home for every story, with sources linked at the bottom of each post.
