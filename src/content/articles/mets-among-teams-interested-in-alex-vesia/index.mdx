The market for Alex Vesia is heating up.

Per **Will Sammon**, the Mets are among the teams interested in **Alex Vesia** and **Tim Hill**. New York, like just about every club, is looking to strengthen its bullpen this winter.

## Vesia's 2026

Vesia, a Dodgers free agent, had a 2.64 ERA with 74 strikeouts in 58 innings over a career-high 72 appearances in 2026.

## Who else is in

The Marlins are also expected to pursue him, per Kevin Barral of Fish on First. Spotrac projects a two-year, $14.2 million deal.
