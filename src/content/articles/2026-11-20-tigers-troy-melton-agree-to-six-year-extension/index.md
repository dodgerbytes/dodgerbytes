The Tigers and right-hander Troy Melton have agreed to a six-year, $46.5MM contract extension, per ESPN’s Jeff Passan. The deal includes a club option for 2033 and can max out at $98MM with incentives. Melton is a client of Paragon Sports International. More to come.
