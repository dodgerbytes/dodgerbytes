The Dodgers have a new hitting coach.

LA hired **Pedro Guerrero** away from the Marlins, where he was the hitting coach for the last two seasons. The 37-year-old will now work with Shohei Ohtani and the rest of the Dodgers lineup.

Not to be confused with the former Dodgers slugger of the same name, the 1981 World Series co-MVP.
