The Mets are making their move on the biggest name in free agency.

Per **Jon Heyman**, the Mets have a meeting scheduled with **Tarik Skubal**, and the two sides will sit down this week.

## What it means for the Dodgers

Skubal, a two-time Cy Young winner, is the top free agent on the market, and the Dodgers have been linked to him all offseason. A sit-down with New York is a sign the Mets are serious, and it could push the timeline on his decision.
