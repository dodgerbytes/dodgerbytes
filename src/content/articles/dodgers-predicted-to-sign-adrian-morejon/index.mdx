This one is a **prediction, not a report**, but it's a big one.

MLB Trade Rumors' **Darragh McDonald** projects the Dodgers to land free-agent left-hander **Adrian Morejon** from the Padres on a **five-year, $80 million** contract, about $16 million a year. Morejon is No. 8 on MLBTR's annual Top 50 Free Agents list.

## Why the Dodgers make sense

Age is a big part of Morejon's appeal. He's 27 and won't turn 28 until February, which makes him one of the youngest free agents on the market. As McDonald put it, teams are buying "two to three more prime seasons with him" than with a typical free agent entering his age-30 or age-31 season.

Taking a top lefty from an NL West rival would sting San Diego twice.
