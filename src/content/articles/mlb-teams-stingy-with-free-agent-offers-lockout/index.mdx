Free agency is open, but the offers aren't flowing.

Per **Jon Heyman of the New York Post**, agents say MLB teams are holding back free-agent offers as a lockout looms, with the collective bargaining agreement set to expire in early December. One agent predicted mostly one-year deals, the kind teams can get out of if games are lost. Bleacher Nation reports that almost no offers have gone out yet.

## What it means for the Dodgers

It's the same uncertainty behind reports that the Dodgers prefer short-term deals this winter. Until the new rules are clear, expect a slow market.
